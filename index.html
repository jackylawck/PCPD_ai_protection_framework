100% 決定性預審硬化
Zero LLM Hallucination

管轄邊界自動硬性攔截
Boundary Enforcement Guardrails

精準偵測非私隱條例管轄範圍（如勞工法例、性別歧視、版權歸屬），自動觸發拒絕評估機制，維護管治審計的嚴謹性。
Hardcoded refusal mechanisms intercept out-of-scope inquiries to maintain legal precision and risk integrity.

密碼學審計軌跡紀錄
Cryptographic SHA-256 Audit Trail

每筆預審查詢即時生成對齊 ISO 42001 之 SHA-256 哈希值與 UTC 時間戳，確保企業內部 AI 評估紀錄具備不可篡改性與可審計性。
Generates unique SHA-256 audit hashes for every compliance check, supporting non-repudiation and ISO 42001 verification.

🛡️ ISO 42001 & Privacy-by-Design 治理架構

🔒 零數據留存 (Zero Data Retention / ZDR)

採用純 Session-Only 記憶體運算架構。用戶輸入之預審情境絕不儲存於任何硬碟或雲端資料庫，會話關閉即刻徹底銷毀。
Session-only in-memory processing. User scenarios are evaluated dynamically and destroyed instantly upon session closure.

🔒 數據最小化與 PII 隔離 (Data Minimization)

系統僅接收管理情境描述，設計上徹底隔離個人識別資訊 (PII)。後台日誌僅儲存不可逆之 SHA-256 哈希值，完全對齊私隱條例。
Ingests scenario descriptions only, strictly isolating PII. Audit logs preserve privacy by storing cryptographic hashes only.
